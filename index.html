About the Cane Corso

An ancient Italian guardian breed — powerful, loyal and intelligent.

Temperament

Devoted and affectionate with family, naturally protective and reserved with strangers. Confident, eager to work and highly trainable with consistent leadership.

Size

A large, muscular breed. Adult males typically stand about 25–27.5 in. and females about 23.5–26 in. at the shoulder, often weighing roughly 90–120+ lbs.

Care

Short coat with low grooming needs, but significant daily exercise and mental stimulation. Typical lifespan is about 9–12 years. Quality nutrition and joint-conscious growth matter.

An honest note: A Cane Corso is not for everyone. They need an experienced, committed owner, early and ongoing socialization, structured training, and a secure home. If you're a first-time large-breed owner, talk with us before applying.
